# Independent Review Protocol v1

*Blinded pairwise review of reconstructed Full Stack v4 evaluation cases*

> Work in progress | September 2026  
> Protocol prepared. Independent review has not yet been completed.

## Status and Role

This is a portfolio-level evaluation protocol.

It is **not part of the canonical Full Stack v4 architecture** and does not modify the private Operating Manual or Execution Prompt.

Its purpose is to move the repository from author-adjudicated reconstructed examples toward more structured testing through independent domain review.

The protocol uses the current public Evaluation Approach as its methodological basis.

## What This Round Tests

Each reviewer receives the same frozen case packet and two candidate reasoning responses.

The reviewer is not told which response used Full Stack v4.

The review asks whether one response is materially stronger on reasoning quality and decision usefulness, not whether one is longer, more polished, more detailed, or more sophisticated in tone.

The seven evaluation dimensions are

| Dimension | Review question |
| --- | --- |
| **Evidence discipline** | Which response better separates supported facts and observations from inference, assumptions, and recommendations? |
| **Competing explanations** | Which response handles credible alternatives more appropriately before accepting a diagnosis? |
| **Causal diagnosis** | Which response better distinguishes the visible symptom from the condition materially governing the outcome? |
| **Prognosis** | Which response better explains what the current condition is likely to produce and what could change that trajectory? |
| **Pressure testing** | Which conclusion better survives credible counterarguments, missing context, and disconfirming evidence? |
| **Confidence calibration** | Which response better matches confidence to the strength of the evidence and causal logic? |
| **Decision usefulness** | Which response better clarifies what should be believed, investigated, or done? |

Decision usefulness matters, but it does not compensate for unsupported inference or weaker causal reasoning.

## Review Design

Pack v1 contains five reconstructed cases already used in the public portfolio.

For this review round

- the decision question and frozen case packet are preserved
- the author-adjudicated outcome is withheld
- the condition labels are replaced with **Response A** and **Response B**
- response order is balanced and randomized across the five cases
- the reviewer is asked not to consult the public repository until the review is complete
- reviewer disagreement is preserved rather than forced into consensus
- existing case classifications remain unchanged until independent results are recorded and reviewed

Perfect blinding is not guaranteed.

The two reasoning conditions can differ in length and structure, and a reviewer already familiar with Full Stack may recognize characteristics of the architecture.

Reviewer familiarity is therefore recorded rather than ignored.

## Reviewer Qualification

The preferred reviewer understands consequential GTM or executive operating decisions well enough to judge whether a diagnosis and intervention would survive contact with a real business.

The reviewer does not need to be an AI specialist.

Relevant experience may include executive GTM leadership, sales leadership, revenue operations, strategy, operating advisory work, or another role with direct responsibility for the kinds of decisions represented in the cases.

## Reviewer Instructions

Review each case independently.

Use only the evidence in the frozen case packet.

Do not add facts that are not provided.

Do not reward a response merely because it is longer or contains more analytical steps.

Do not reward disagreement with the other response merely because it appears more nuanced.

For each case

1. Read the decision question and frozen case packet.
2. Read Response A and Response B.
3. Assess the seven dimensions.
4. Choose an overall pairwise judgment.
5. State the strongest reason the preferred response could still be wrong.
6. Identify missing evidence that could materially change the decision.
7. Record confidence in your judgment.

The overall pairwise choices are

- **Response A materially stronger**
- **Response B materially stronger**
- **No material difference**
- **Indeterminate from the available evidence**

These are reviewer judgments before unblinding.

They are not yet the repository's Full Stack outcome classifications.

## Reviewer Independence Record

Before reviewing, record

- role or domain
- approximate level of GTM or executive operating experience
- whether you have previously seen these public cases
- whether you are familiar with Full Stack v4
- whether you have a professional relationship with the framework author

This information is used to interpret possible bias or prior exposure.

It does not automatically disqualify a reviewer.

## Unblinding and Interpretation

After a reviewer submits all five cases, the internal mapping can be revealed.

The pairwise result can then be translated into the existing working outcome categories.

| Independent result after unblinding | Repository interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Full Stack response materially stronger | **Improved** |
| Baseline response materially stronger | **Degraded** |
| No material difference | **No material change** |
| Reviewer cannot distinguish from available evidence | **Indeterminate** |

The research record should preserve the original author adjudication and the independent adjudication separately.

A disagreement does not automatically overwrite either one.

It becomes evidence to inspect.

## Multiple Reviewers

If more than one reviewer participates, preserve individual judgments first.

Do not manufacture consensus.

A later aggregate summary may report patterns such as

- reviewer agreement
- reviewer disagreement
- dimensions where disagreement concentrates
- cases where prior Full Stack familiarity may have affected blinding
- cases where reviewers identify missing evidence not anticipated in the author adjudication

Pack v1 does not define a statistical validation threshold.

The current objective is structured independent judgment, not a quantitative reliability claim.

## What This Protocol Can Support

A completed independent review round can strengthen the evidence that

- the cases are not evaluated only by their author
- negative and unresolved judgments remain admissible
- the same public criteria can be applied by another experienced operator
- disagreement can be documented rather than explained away

It cannot by itself establish formal validation, generalizability, or a performance rate for Full Stack v4.

## Version Discipline

The cases are frozen for the review round.

Do not revise Full Stack or rewrite a case while that case is being independently evaluated.

If review reveals a possible framework improvement, record it separately as

- no permanent change
- working hypothesis
- proposed framework modification

Any canonical framework change requires separate approval and version discipline.

## Public and Private Boundary

The protocol can be public.

The active blinded case pack, response mapping, reviewer identities, and unreleased reviewer judgments should remain private during the review round.

After review, selected results can be published with appropriate consent and claim discipline.

## Current Position

Independent Review Pack v1 is a step toward **structured testing**.

It does not change the current evidence status by itself.

The evidence level changes only when independent reviews are actually completed, preserved, and assessed.
