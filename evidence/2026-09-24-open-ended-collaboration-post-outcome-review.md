## Why this entry exists

[Evidence Entry 001](2026-09-14-open-ended-collaboration.md) froze the reasoning state before the external contribution was available for review.

Its working hypothesis was not that two architectures would necessarily converge.

The hypothesis was narrower.

> **A useful indicator of epistemic integrity may be whether credible new evidence is allowed to change the preferred conclusion.**

The earlier entry also defined evidence that would strengthen or weaken that interpretation.

It would become more meaningful if the participants preserved an external contribution independently, rejected an attractive integration when the boundary did not survive scrutiny, or changed the existing framework only after a genuine reasoning gap was demonstrated.

It would weaken if later discussion began protecting convergence or if an external contribution were absorbed without preserving attribution and independent review.

The later evidence now exists.

Spark Tsai submitted [PR #29](https://github.com/sschoenfeld-scotty/gtm-ai-reasoning-systems/pull/29), **Add Evidence Center provenance reference architecture**.

This entry compares that outcome with the frozen prior state without changing Evidence Entry 001.

## What arrived

PR #29 is independently authored by Spark Tsai.

PR #29 was later closed without merge on September 24, 2026 after the architectural review and contributor clarification were complete.

The PR adds one proposed research file and does not modify existing Full Stack architecture, evaluation, evidence, or canonical files.

Spark describes the Evidence Center as an independent reference architecture for preserving evidence states and provenance without deciding what the evidence means.

Its proposed boundary separates preservation from interpretation.

Full Stack appears as a mapping case rather than as the source architecture being modified.

## What the review found

The contribution is substantive enough to make the earlier overlap concrete.

Full Stack and the Evidence Center both care about preserving distinctions that can disappear over time, but they govern different responsibilities.

Current canonical Full Stack already contains Decision Trace Integrity and Recursive Evidence Re-entry.

Those disciplines govern what reasoning state should be preserved when material, how later evidence should re-enter the reasoning process, and how the current model may change without silently rewriting the earlier state.

PR #29 addresses a different problem.

It proposes a provenance layer for preserving outcome states, content identity, actor provenance, external references, and explicitly asserted relationships without interpreting the reasoning semantics inside the preserved artifact.

That is an adjacent infrastructure problem rather than a missing Full Stack reasoning function.

## Effect on the frozen working hypothesis

The later outcome is consistent with the earlier working hypothesis and modestly strengthens it.

The external contribution was preserved as independently authored work.

The review did not treat architectural overlap as sufficient reason for integration.

The contribution was compared against the current canonical Full Stack sources before any framework change was considered.

The current decision is still no Full Stack modification.

Those outcomes match the kinds of later evidence that Evidence Entry 001 identified in advance as potentially strengthening the interpretation.

This remains one observed case.

It does not validate a general claim about epistemic integrity, objectivity, or reasoning quality.

## Architectural boundary after review

The new evidence makes the boundary more precise.

Full Stack remains responsible for reasoning and judgment.

The Evidence Center remains an independently authored provenance reference architecture.

A provenance layer could plausibly preserve artifacts produced or consumed by a reasoning system without becoming part of the reasoning architecture itself.

The overlap is therefore real enough to map while the separation remains useful.

PR #29 did not demonstrate that Full Stack is missing a peer capability, a nested refinement, or a new version requirement.

## Behavioral Inference Engine implication

The Evidence Center may be relevant to future BIE work because BIE still has an unresolved longitudinal persistence problem.

Behavioral observations and later contradictory evidence may need to persist across time without rewriting earlier states.

A provenance layer could potentially preserve those states.

It would not decide whether later evidence strengthens a behavioral hypothesis, represents an outlier, reflects changed context, or requires model revision.

That reasoning remains BIE's responsibility.

The current evidence therefore supports a possible future infrastructure relationship, not a BIE architecture change.

## Authorship and repository boundary

Spark Tsai remains the independent author of the Evidence Center architecture proposed in PR #29.

This evidence entry records Scotty Schoenfeld's review of the contribution and its implications for the existing reasoning systems.

It does not reproduce Spark's proposed architecture or present that work as Scotty's intellectual property.

The current repository decision is to preserve the review and link to the external contribution rather than absorb the contribution into the canonical reasoning architecture.

## Final repository disposition

After the review, Spark clarified that he had primarily used the PR as a vehicle for independent review rather than assuming the Evidence Center should be merged into the repository.

He was also comfortable with future inclusion as independently authored adjacent research if the architectural boundary and authorship remained explicit.

The repository decision was therefore made separately from the architectural judgment.

For now, PR #29 is closed without merge.

The reason is portfolio coherence and timing.

The repository is still early enough that adding a large independently authored adjacent architecture could blur the center of gravity before the existing body of work has enough context around it.

This is not a rejection of the Evidence Center architecture.

The decision can be revisited if the repository reaches a point where independently authored adjacent research can be included without diluting the story of the reasoning systems themselves.

## What would change this conclusion

The Full Stack boundary should be reconsidered if later use shows that Decision Trace Integrity cannot operate adequately without reasoning-owned provenance functions.

The BIE boundary should be reconsidered if longitudinal inference later requires persistence semantics that cannot remain cleanly separated from model revision.

Neither condition has been demonstrated by PR #29.

## Current synthesis

The later contribution did not prove that two approaches should converge into one architecture.

It did something more useful.

It gave the earlier reasoning a real outcome to confront.

The contribution made the overlap concrete while making the boundary clearer.

The prior working hypothesis survived the new evidence and gained some support, while the framework itself remained unchanged.

Reality changed the precision of the model without rewriting the reasoning state that existed before the evidence arrived.
