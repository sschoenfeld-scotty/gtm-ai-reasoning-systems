# Instruction Architecture

*The operating responsibilities around my reasoning systems*

A reasoning method can be well specified and still be used under the wrong conditions. An old summary can stand in for the current source. A request for analysis can be mistaken for permission to act. A document labeled canonical can be treated as if the AI host must obey it.

I developed the instruction architecture around my reasoning systems to make these failures easier to identify. As an enterprise GTM executive, I need to know what supports a recommendation and what work was actually done before I stand behind it.

An approved private operating guide explains these relationships. This page gives a public account of the design. The guide's approval establishes its explanatory role, not tested execution reliability.

## Different responsibilities need different homes

The reasoning method and the environment in which it is used have different responsibilities.

| Responsibility | What it governs | Risk of putting it elsewhere |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account or deployment baseline | Integrity expectations that should remain available across tasks | A safeguard disappears when a new task omits it |
| Project policy | Workstream scope and boundaries on information use | A project's assumptions or disclosure rules are applied to unrelated work |
| Framework sources | The selected method's reasoning requirements | A shortened copy becomes a competing version of the method |
| Task instructions | The present decision and permission to act | A temporary request becomes a standing rule, or analysis becomes unauthorized action |
| Supporting records | Evidence and context within their stated scope | A historical observation becomes a current instruction |

This table describes intended responsibilities. It is not an instruction-precedence ladder. Host rules and tool permissions constrain the whole arrangement.

Personal writing preferences can be replaced without changing a method. Evidence requirements cannot be replaced by a preference for a more confident answer. Editorial choices belong after substantive reasoning and must preserve its qualifications. Publication requires its own authorized scope.

## Source authority does not establish runtime priority

Source authority answers which document the owner has designated to govern a method. Runtime precedence concerns which instructions the host actually prioritizes when requirements compete.

A canonical document does not gain system-level priority from its title. A project policy that extends an account baseline does not prove that the host resolves conflicts in that order. The receiving environment needs its own check of what is available and what can be enforced.

The same boundary applies to execution claims. Describing a method is different from applying it. Reading a source alone does not establish that the method was followed. If a required source or capability is unavailable, the dependent execution claim must remain unmade. Any separately authorized preliminary work needs an accurate label.

## Full Stack and GTM keep their own jobs

[Full Stack v5.1](./full-stack-v5.1-public-architecture.md) addresses diagnosis and strategic judgment across decision contexts. [GTM Diagnostic Framework v9](./gtm-diagnostic-framework-v9-public-architecture.md) addresses commercial constraints and operating intervention.

They remain separately governed methods. Neither is a required stage of the other. When both contribute, each needs a defined decision job. Agreement based on shared evidence does not establish independent confirmation.

The instruction environment does not supply another [Logic Lens](../docs/what-is-a-logic-lens.md). It explains the conditions around method use. The human operator remains accountable for the judgment and any authorized action.

## A reconstructed commercial example

A team reports falling conversion and asks AI to diagnose the problem using GTM. Its preferred response is more prospecting activity. The task authorizes analysis only.

The potential failure is to accept the proposed activity increase as the answer, use remembered method language and present a plan as a completed GTM diagnosis. That would hide both the source gap and the untested explanation.

The design calls for the activity proposal to remain open to challenge and for any claim of method use to be verified. If the governing source is unavailable, the claimed GTM execution stops. If it is available, commercial evidence still has to establish whether pipeline creation or buyer progression is the constraint.

The reasoning decision changes from producing an activity plan to withholding that recommendation until the evidence supports it. More activity remains an available conclusion. A different buyer dependency could instead warrant attention. Neither conclusion grants permission to install an intervention.

This scenario is reconstructed. It contains no company evidence and reports no completed evaluation or measured business result.

## What can transfer, and what remains untested

These responsibilities can be explained without my personal account instructions or accumulated conversation history. That makes them candidates for deployment elsewhere. It does not establish equivalent behavior on another host.

Another environment would need to demonstrate access to its authorized sources and correct handling of conflicting instructions. Unsupported capabilities and failures would need to remain visible. Copying instruction text is insufficient evidence of equivalent execution.

The contribution described here is the assignment of operating responsibilities around human-directed reasoning. It is not a claim to have invented platform instruction hierarchy or built a production-tested software system. The existence of the guide establishes neither framework efficacy nor commercial benefit.

An [earlier instruction-architecture audit](../evidence/2026-09-27-full-stack-assisted-instruction-architecture-audit.md) records two fresh-context reviews of a September instruction artifact. Those reviews concern that earlier artifact. They did not test the October operating guide, measure future compliance or establish that Full Stack caused the reviewed artifact's quality.
